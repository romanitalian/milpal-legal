**Effective date:** November 22, 2026

**Email:** rmndevapps@gmail.com

## About Milpal

**Milpal** ("the app") helps English speakers learn the 4,000 most useful Spanish words at CEFR levels A1-B2, using spaced repetition, audio and verb practice. It is available for iOS and Android.

The app does not require an account or sign-in. Core study works without an internet connection.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally:

- study level, learning goal and onboarding state;
- spaced-repetition progress for each card;
- lesson and review statistics;
- app settings, such as theme and audio preferences.

We do not operate a server that receives this learning data. Deleting the app normally deletes this local data. If you use **Progress backup**, the app creates a file that you choose where to save or share.

## Information we do not collect

- Your name, email address, phone number or mailing address inside the app.
- The individual words you study or your per-card progress.
- Your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA or Android Advertising ID).
- Your payment card details.

We do not sell your personal information, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

## Product analytics

We use **PostHog** (European Union hosting) to understand how the app is used and to improve it.

- **Events:** app lifecycle, onboarding completed, learning goal and starting level, first rating, lesson completed, daily limit progress, verb practice results, paywall views, and purchase, cancellation and restore outcomes.
- **Technical data:** a random anonymous identifier created by the app, app version, build type, platform, device model, operating system, language, and whether you are on the free or Pro plan.

Session replay is turned off.

## Subscription management

We use **RevenueCat** to manage subscription status. It receives the same random anonymous identifier and purchase information, such as product, price, currency, store country, and renewal or cancellation status.

## Crash reporting

We use **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google) to diagnose crashes. A crash report includes technical data such as the stack trace, app version, device model, operating system, device state, the random anonymous identifier, a Firebase installation identifier, and your plan type.

## Network data

When the app connects to these services, or to Apple or Google, they may process your IP address as part of the connection.

## Subscriptions and store purchases

Paid subscriptions are billed by **Apple** or **Google** through your store account. They handle payment, billing, renewals, cancellations and refunds under their own terms. We do not receive your full payment details.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage them in your Apple or Google Play account settings.

Optional audio packs are delivered through Apple On-Demand Resources or Google Play Asset Delivery.

## Why we process data

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

- **performance of a contract** to provide and manage subscriptions;
- **legitimate interests** to keep the app reliable, understand feature use and improve learning.

## Service providers and international transfers

We share data only with the providers named in this policy and with Apple or Google as app stores. Some providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.

## Data retention

Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app. Analytics, subscription and crash data are kept by the providers for as long as needed for the purposes above, according to the retention settings and legal obligations that apply.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. In Portugal, this is the CNPD.

Because the app has no accounts, we may need information from you to locate data linked to your anonymous identifier. Contact **rmndevapps@gmail.com** with your request.

## California residents

We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined in California privacy law.

## Children

Milpal is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will address it.
